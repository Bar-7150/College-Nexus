Every document uploaded to College Nexus is hashed on the client side using SHA-256 cryptography. If an identical file exists anywhere in the archive, the system rejects it immediately with duplicate prevention warnings. Furthermore, appointed Class Representatives (CRs) review all papers.
When a student finds a misplaced item (e.g., calculator, drafter, keys), they upload it with a hidden 'Verification Question'. Claimants answer this prompt without their personal phone numbers or social media accounts ever being broadcast publicly. Handover occurs directly at the student council or canteen.
While general notice circulars and university archives are publicly indexable for academic research, interactive actions such as claiming lost items, listing marketplace gear, and rating notes require authenticating via KGEC roll numbers.
